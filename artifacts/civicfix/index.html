A CLEARER WAY TO THINK IT THROUGH — 02

From “someone
should” to where to start.

Useful civic information starts with an observation. A little context can make it easier to understand what is happening and what could help.

01 / NOTICE

Start with what you saw.

Describe the issue in plain, specific terms. Stick to details someone else could verify.

02 / ADD CONTEXT

Make the details useful.

A nearby landmark, timing, or clear description can help explain why an issue matters.

03 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Look for a next step.

Local knowledge can point toward the right people, resources, or community conversation.

Ask CivicAI.

Get practical suggestions for understanding a local issue and finding a useful next step. CivicAI is not a government service and does not submit or save reports.

Messages are sent to the configured AI provider for a response and are not stored by CivicFix. Avoid names and exact addresses; verify advice with official local sources.
